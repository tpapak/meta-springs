The binomial observation spring is nonlinear

One arm, two likelihoods on the same log-odds axis ℓ = xt − xτ. Gaussian spring: restoring force (y−ℓ)/v grows without bound as you pull away. Binomial spring: restoring force e − nσ(ℓ) saturates in [e−n, e] — soft in the tails, stiff near p=½. Drag either treatment node (or the slider); both share the same ℓ. At the mode they linearise to the same Hookean spring (Fisher-information stiffness); away from it they diverge. Press Relax (IRLS) to let the binomial spring find equilibrium by repeated linearisation.
