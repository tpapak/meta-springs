Fixed-effect NMA as a spring network

A synthetic 4-treatment continuous network (4 studies). Each study starts on its own, its observation springs at their natural length — the raw within-study effects, no tension. Press Link studies: the shared treatment nodes are glued into one column each, the springs stretch, and the network settles into the inverse-variance fixed-effect estimate. Stiffer (more precise) studies pull harder.
