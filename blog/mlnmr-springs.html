ML-NMR: an IPD study and an aggregate study with a patient-level covariate

Binary outcome, logit link, fixed effects: logit p = μj + β1(x−x̄) + dk + β2(x−x̄), with dA = 0 and no interaction on A (multinma notation). The vertical axis in each study row is the patient covariate x. Each study is a study rod tilted by the prognostic slope β1; each treatment is a treatment rod, glued through both rows, tilted by the interaction β2. Study 1 shares IPD (A vs B): one spring per patient. Study 2 reports only arm totals and the covariate mean and SD (A vs C): one spring per arm, spread over its covariate distribution.
SD = 0.80
Horizontal = linear predictor (log-odds). A spring's horizontal length, from the study rod to the treatment rod, is that patient's or arm's log-odds η. Study rods sit at −μj and tilt by β1; they share the tilt but not the position. Treatment rods sit at dk and tilt by β2; they are glued through both studies (A pinned and vertical). Tilts pivot at x̄. The dashed lines show where each rod would stand untilted. IPD patients are thin springs at their own height: responders push η up, non-responders push it down (a binary spring has no rest length of its own). Aggregate arms are one sheathed spring each: sub-springs at the integration heights of the reported covariate distribution, whose probabilities are averaged before one binomial energy. At mean covariate replaces the sheath by one spring at the mean height (aggregation bias). Set the SD to 0 and the two coincide.
