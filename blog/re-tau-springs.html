Random effects: a τ-spring per arm

The random-effects model gives every arm its own slack link. In series, the observation spring (stiffness 1/vij) and a τ-spring collapse into one effective spring of stiffness keff = 1/(vij + τ²/2): adding τ²/2 in series is literal mechanical shrinkage. Each study's spindle Si sits at its centroid μ̂i; each arm runs a τ-chain out to its ζ̂ node (the equilibrium τ-spring length), then a blue obs spring lands on its treatment column dt. Raise τ² and the chains lengthen, over-precise studies get down-weighted, and the columns slide from the fixed-effect fit (τ²=0) toward the random-effects estimate.
