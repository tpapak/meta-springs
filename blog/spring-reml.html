Spring-REML: heterogeneity as a mean-square length

A Gaussian network with arm deviations ζij ~ N(0, τ²/2). Here τ² is contrast heterogeneity. At each selected τ², the network supplies the BLUP ζ̂ and its conditional variance Vζ, including uncertainty in the fitted study and treatment means. The bars show (2/N)Σζ̂² and (2/N)ΣVζ. Their sum is the next EM estimate of τ²; Solve REML finds the fixed point.
