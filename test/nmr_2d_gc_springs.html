2-D grand canonical spring network — exchangeable baseline-risk (data-driven: real solve per cell)

Every cell of the lattice is the actual spring solve at that (τ²,σ_B²): the working springs are assembled into A=JᵀW_eff J + R/σ_B², solved for the equilibrium θ̂=A⁻¹b, and scored by the exact log Z — the same computation the Haskell GC runs, in your browser. Cells are coloured by posterior mass ∝ Z·π·π. Hover the lattice: the slope nodes move to their solved positions (welded at small σ_B², spread at large σ_B²) and the τ-chain grows with τ². Dataset: certolizumab.
