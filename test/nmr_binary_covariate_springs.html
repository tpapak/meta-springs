Coloured coils = studies (precision 1/(vi+τ²), natural length = observed log-OR), drawn inside their own covariate layer. Lower band = x = 0, upper band = x = 1. Orange β springs couple each active treatment across the two bands and are forced to share one length — that common displacement is the regression coefficient. The reference A is pinned at effect 0 in both bands, so its β spring stays vertical (covariate shifts effects relative to the reference).
