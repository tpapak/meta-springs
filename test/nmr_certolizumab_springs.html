Network Meta-Regression: Certolizumab in rheumatoid arthritis

Canonical NMR teaching example (Dias et al., NICE DSU TSD 3; gemtc certolizumab). 12 trials, 6 active drugs vs Placebo, outcome ACR50 (log-OR), continuous covariate = mean disease duration (years). Each treatment's effect-vs-covariate line is a spring-loaded regression: every study (dot) hangs from its treatment's line by a vertical spring of stiffness 1/(vi+τ²). Shared-slope model: one common β.

Coloured lines = each drug's log-OR vs placebo as a function of disease duration, y = dt + β·x. Dots = observed trial log-ORs (0.5 continuity correction where a cell is 0); coils = the springs pulling each line to its data, thicker = more precise. The dashed line at 0 is placebo (reference, no slope). Toggle Standard NMA to set β=0: the lines go flat and the springs strain wherever effect varies with duration — that strain is the evidence for a covariate effect. The spring equilibrium is the weighted-least-squares NMR fit.
