Continuous covariate: rods held by a παραλληλόγραφος

x = effect (vs A), y = continuous covariate. Each treatment is a rigid rod (its regression line); each study node Si (spindle, at its covariate height) sends one spring per arm to that arm's treatment node (point on the treatment's rod) — including the reference A — exactly as in standard NMA; one study is 4-arm. The παραλληλόγραφος keeps the rods parallel ⇒ one shared tilt = β. β settles by torque balance (lever = covariate height) — no algebra, just forces.

A study (dot) at height xi pulls its rod toward the observed contrast; the rod's torque balance (Σ w·residual·(x−x̄)=0) sets β, lever = covariate offset.
