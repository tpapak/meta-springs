Continuous covariate: rods held by a παραλληλόγραφος

x = effect (vs A), y = continuous covariate. Each treatment is a rigid rod (its regression line); studies sit at their own covariate height and pull the rod horizontally with a spring (natural length = observed contrast). The παραλληλόγραφος keeps the rods parallel ⇒ one shared tilt = β. β settles by torque balance (lever = covariate height) — no algebra, just forces.
