Rigorous arm-level NMR — extending the GC spring network

Golden-standard convention yij = μi + (dj + β·(xi−x̄))·[j≠A]. Each study node Si is a free baseline μi; one obs spring per arm runs from the study node to that arm's treatment node on its rod. μi, dj, β are solved jointly (arm-level WLS) — so active-vs-active studies (no A arm) are handled correctly. β = the shared rod slope (παραλληλόγραφος).
Shortcuts flagged (vs the full GC golden standard): (1) τ springs omitted — fixed-effect only; no heterogeneity/BLUP nodes yet (the GC standard has a τ-chain per arm). (2) Obs-spring geometry is schematic (as in the Ξ animation): the study node is drawn at the centroid of its arms' treatment nodes; the residual is the gap between an arm's observed effect (grey dot) and its fitted treatment node. (3) Multi-arm correlation is handled (shared μi, not independent contrasts) — not a shortcut.
