Exchangeable β is the heterogeneity construct on the slope coordinates

Both panels are the same spring ½·k·(coord − target)² tying a coordinate to a shared node. Top — heterogeneity (random effects): each arm's δ is tied to its study by a τ-spring k=2/τ² ⇒ study effects exchangeable around the network mean. Bottom — exchangeable β: each treatment slope βt is tied to the hyper-mean B by a slope-spring k=1/σ_B² ⇒ slopes exchangeable around B. Identical mechanism, different coordinates; the GC integrates τ² and σ_B² alike.
Solid coils = springs. τ-spring (StudyEnd→TauEnd δ, k=2/τ²) and slope-spring (βt→B, k=1/σ_B²) are drawn identically because they are the same object. Grey coils = obs springs k=1/v. Dashed orange = the covariate lever ×xi (βt shifts the treatment end by βt·xi). ⚓ = pinned reference.
shared β = σ_B²→0 (rigid slope-springs, all βt≡B)  ·  unrelated β = σ_B²→∞ (no slope-springs).
