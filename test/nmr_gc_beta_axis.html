β as a slope on the covariate axis

Two axes: x = effect (log-OR vs A), y = covariate. Each treatment is a line through its two level nodes (b₀ at x=0, b₁ at x=1); the line's horizontal rise per unit covariate is β. Study springs (horizontal) hold the nodes to the data at each covariate height. Shared β = parallel lines. Binary covariate ⇒ two pinned points ⇒ no torque.
β = 0.000
Reference A is the vertical line at effect 0 (flat — no covariate effect). Each other treatment's line passes through its two level nodes; its slope (rise Δeffect over Δcovariate = 1) is β. Shared ⇒ all lines parallel; Unrelated ⇒ each its own slope; Pooled ⇒ vertical lines (β=0). β is read off the geometry — the tilt of data-pinned lines — not a separate spring.
